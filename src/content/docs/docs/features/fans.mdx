**Fans**, in **Tools**, lists the fans Tokio has seen in your LIVEs: your TikTok fan club, your Twitch and Kick subscribers, and your YouTube members.

<Screenshot
  name="fans"
  alt="Tools → Fans: each fan's TikTok badge and level, name and @handle, Super Fan or not, and when Tokio last saw them"
/>

With two or more platforms, each has a tab, then **All**, which lists everyone with the most recently seen first. Each row shows when Tokio last saw the fan.

The search button finds a fan by name or @handle in the tab shown. Fans can't be removed.

## TikTok

The highest fan level comes first and, at the same level, the fan seen most recently. Each row shows the fan's TikTok badge and level, and their name and @handle.

TikTok sends a viewer's fan level only when they do something in the LIVE, like joining, chatting, liking, sending a gift, sharing or following. A fan joins the list the first time they do, and their level and name update each time they come back. One who left your fan club stays listed at their last level.

**Super Fan** after a name marks a fan who subscribes to you. Tokio learns it when they chat, send a gift, become a Super Fan or join your LIVE as one. Turn on **Super Fans only**, on TikTok's tab, to list just them.

## Twitch, YouTube and Kick

Their fans are your subscribers, founders included, and YouTube's are your members. One joins the list when they chat with the subscriber or member badge, or subscribe during a LIVE.

The most months come first, then the fan seen most recently. Each row shows the months, from their chat badge or their subscription, or **Subscriber** (**Member** on YouTube) when Tokio doesn't know them. YouTube's badge only tells its milestone, like a year.

## Overlays

Overlays can show your TikTok fans: they get the 100 highest levels. Subscribers and members stay out. See [Fans](/docs/developers/events/#fans) to build one.

<details>
<summary>How fans are saved</summary>

Each profile keeps its own fans on your computer, up to 5,000 per platform. Past that, the fan Tokio hasn't seen for the longest goes.

Tokio saves them every 30 seconds and when a LIVE ends.

Exports leave fans out, and a cloned or imported profile starts with none.

</details>
