Four controls work from a key or your phone while Tokio stays in the background, so you don't have to switch windows during your LIVE.

<Screenshot
  name="remote"
  alt="The remote on a phone: the LIVE's @username, the song playing, Skip song, Stop TTS, Replay last alert and Pause everything"
/>

| Control               | What it does                                                                                                                             |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Skip song**         | Plays the next song request, or the player's own next song with none waiting.                                                            |
| **Stop TTS**          | Stops the chat message being read and drops the ones waiting.                                                                            |
| **Replay last alert** | Plays the last event's alerts again, once, even with alerts turned off.                                                                  |
| **Pause everything**  | Stops TTS and alerts, and ignores chat TTS, alerts, song requests and game actions until you resume. Nothing from the pause plays later. |

## Shortcuts

In **Settings → Shortcuts**, click a control's field and press the keys. **Backspace** removes a shortcut, and **Esc** cancels.

<Screenshot
  name="settings-shortcuts"
  alt="Settings → Shortcuts, with F13, F14, F15 and F16 for the four controls"
/>

A shortcut works in every app, and the other apps lose those keys, so Tokio only takes combinations they rarely use:

- **F13** to **F24** and media keys like **Play/Pause** work alone. Macro pads and Stream Deck hotkey actions send them. A media key you pick stops working in your other players.
- Any other key needs **Ctrl** and one more modifier, like **Ctrl + Shift + S**. On macOS, that's **Command** or **Control** and one more.
- On Windows and Linux, **Ctrl + Alt** also needs **Shift**, since it types characters like @ and € on many keyboards.

When another app already uses one, Tokio tells you and shows **In use by another app** under it: pick a different one.

## Pause

While Tokio is paused, Home says so at the top. Click **Resume** there, or press the shortcut again. The music, the countdown and your overlays carry on. Connecting to a LIVE or restarting Tokio resumes too.

## From your phone or another computer

In **Settings → Remote control**, check **Enable remote control** and click **Show QR code and key**. Scan the code with a phone on the same Wi-Fi, or open the **Link** on another computer on your network. Bookmark the page to open it again.

<Screenshot
  name="settings-remote"
  alt="Remote control turned on, with the QR code, the link and the key"
/>

The page shows the LIVE, the song playing and when Tokio is paused. A control with nothing to do is grayed out and says why.

It reaches Tokio through the overlay server, so check **Enable server** in **Settings → Overlay server** too. If Windows asks whether to allow Tokio through the firewall, allow it, or the page [can't connect](/docs/guides/adding-an-overlay/#on-another-computer).

Anyone on your network with the link can use the controls. While remote control is on, the button next to its title makes a new key, after asking.

Devices with the old link stop working until you open the new one. To press the controls from a Stream Deck or a script, see [Control API](/docs/developers/control-api/).

AI assistants have their own section, **Settings → AI assistants** ([MCP](/docs/developers/mcp/)).
