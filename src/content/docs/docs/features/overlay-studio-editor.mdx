The editor opens when you click an overlay in [Overlay Studio](/docs/features/overlay-studio/). Each change saves as you make it and reaches your stream right away.

## Elements and groups

The **Elements** list shows every element, the front one on top, with a small picture of what it shows. An element that reacts to an event says which one under its name.

<Screenshot
  name="studio-elements"
  alt="The Elements list: the logo on its own, a group with a viewer's picture, the follow message and its speech bubble, and a group with the star, the likes text and the banner's rectangle, each with a small picture of what it shows"
/>

In the **+** menu, **New group** adds a group in front of the rest.

A new element goes into the selected group, or the selected element's group, and the menu names it: **Add to Group 1**. Otherwise it goes in front, on its own.

Drag a row's grip to reorder groups and elements, or to move an element into a group or out of one. Where you drop an element decides where it goes:

| Drop it                                  | It goes                           |
| ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| On a group's row                         | In front, in that group           |
| Near the top edge of a group's row       | In front of the group, on its own |
| Below the list, or in a gap between rows | There, on its own                 |

You can also focus a grip and press the up and down arrows. An element at the edge of its group steps out of it, and an element on its own steps into a group it meets.

Each group's row has three buttons:

- the arrow next to its grip folds its elements away in the list;
- **Lock group** keeps the canvas from picking, moving, resizing or rotating its elements, and a click goes through to what's under them. You can still change them from the list and their settings;
- **Hide group** takes it off the canvas and the stream until you show it again.

Each element's row has its own **Lock element** and **Hide element**, which work the same for that element alone. The lock and hide buttons show when you point at a row or select it, and stay visible while they're on.

Select a group or an element to name it in its settings. An element without a name shows what it is, like its text.

To delete the selection, click the trash button next to **+**. A group goes with its elements, and **Undo** brings them back.

## On the canvas

Click an element to select it, drag it to move it and drag any of its corners to resize it. To rotate it, drag the round handle above it.

A text's box can't get narrower than its longest word, or shorter than its words. It grows to fit them when you change its text, font, size or bold.

**Position and size**, **Rotation (°)** and **Opacity** set the same things by number. You can also click and hold a number field, then drag left or right to change its number.

**Link width and height**, the button between **Width** and **Height**, keeps the proportions as you change either one, in the fields or by dragging a corner. **Align to the canvas** moves the element to an edge of the canvas or to its center, and **Flip** mirrors it horizontally or vertically.

Select a group in the list to change all its elements at once. Drag inside its outline to move it, and drag its corners or its round handle to resize or rotate it.

A group's settings have the same fields as an element's, and its **Opacity** fades the whole group. Click an empty spot on the canvas or in the list to deselect.

## Sticking

When you drag an element, a group or a corner, it sticks to the canvas's edges and middle, the other elements' edges and middles, and the guides while the rulers are on. A line shows what it lines up with.

Hold Alt while you drag to place it freely. The arrow keys, the fields, **Align to the canvas** and rotating never stick.

## Cropping

You can crop an image, a video, a camera, a gift or an emote. The whole picture shows while you crop, with the parts you cut away dimmed.

<Steps>

1. Double-click the element, or click **Crop on the canvas** in its settings.
2. Drag the frame's sides or corners to crop it, or drag inside the frame to move the picture under it.
3. Press Enter or click outside to keep the crop, or press Escape to cancel.

</Steps>

<Screenshot
  name="studio-crop"
  alt="Cropping the logo on the canvas: all of it shows, the parts cut away dimmed around a frame with a handle on each side and corner"
/>

Cropping never moves or resizes the picture: the element's box shrinks to the part you keep. **Crop (%)** in its settings sets the same crop by number, and **Reset the crop** brings back the whole picture.

**Round corners** rounds them, up to a circle for a square picture. A new file, gift or emote starts uncropped.

A viewer's picture crops from its fields only. They pick the part of each viewer's picture that fills its circle.

## Videos

**Cut** keeps a part of a video, as an [alert's cut](/docs/features/alerts/#cutting-a-sound) does: drag the handles, or type the **Start** and **End** seconds. The canvas shows the frame you're at, then plays the cut again, and **Reset the cut** brings back the whole video.

**Volume** sets how loud it plays. With **Loop** on, the video starts over when its cut ends.

With **Loop** off, the video plays once, and an element that shows on an event leaves when it ends: its **Seconds on screen** reads **Until the video ends**. **Play again**, next to **Loop**, plays it once more on the canvas.

An element that moves on an event plays its video again each time. The canvas is silent while you edit: click **Canvas sound**, the speaker under the canvas, to hear its videos.

## Copy, paste and undo

Copy, cut and paste work on the selection, each paste 20 px down and to the right of the last. A copied element goes into the selected group, and a copied group pastes as a new group.

The copy stays until Tokio quits, so you can paste it into another overlay.

**Undo** and **Redo**, at the top of the editor, go back and forward through your changes while the editor is open. A drag on the canvas counts as one change, and so does a number typed into a field.

Folding a group, showing the rulers, the selection and the zoom aren't changes. Each change to the guides is one.

## Zoom

The bar under the canvas shows its zoom level. Zoom with its buttons, or with Ctrl and the mouse wheel where the pointer is.

**Zoom to fit** shows the whole canvas again. Zoomed in, move around with the wheel or the scrollbars, or hold Space and drag the canvas.

## Rulers and guides

**Rulers and guides**, the button beside the zoom, shows rulers along the canvas's top and left edges, in pixels from its top-left corner. Each overlay remembers whether they're on.

<Screenshot
  name="studio-guides"
  alt="The canvas with rulers along its top and left edges and a guide across each of its middles"
/>

Hold Ctrl and drag from the top ruler for a horizontal guide, or from the left one for a vertical guide. Hold Ctrl and drag a guide to move it, or back onto a ruler to remove it.

With nothing selected, **Overlay settings** lists the **Guides**: type their positions, add one at the canvas's middle, lock them or remove them all. Guides are saved with the overlay and never show on stream.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Ctrl is Cmd on a Mac, and Alt is Option. While you type in a field, its keys work on its text.

| Keys                         | What they do                                                                   |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Delete or Backspace          | Delete the selection. On the canvas, a locked one stays                        |
| Escape                       | Deselect, or cancel a crop                                                     |
| Enter                        | Keep a crop                                                                    |
| Arrow keys                   | Move the selection 1 px, 10 px with Shift. On a grip in the list, move its row |
| Shift while rotating         | Turn in steps of 15°                                                           |
| Shift while dragging a field | Change its number 10 at a time                                                 |
| Ctrl+C, Ctrl+X, Ctrl+V       | Copy, cut and paste                                                            |
| Ctrl+Z                       | Undo                                                                           |
| Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z       | Redo                                                                           |
| Ctrl+wheel, or a pinch       | Zoom where the pointer is                                                      |
| Ctrl++ and Ctrl+-            | Zoom in and out                                                                |
| Ctrl+0                       | Zoom to fit                                                                    |
| Space+drag                   | Move around the canvas                                                         |
| Ctrl+R                       | Show or hide the rulers and guides                                             |
| Ctrl+drag from a ruler       | Add a guide                                                                    |
| Ctrl+drag a guide            | Move it, or remove it on a ruler                                               |
| Alt while dragging           | Place it without sticking. On Linux, press Alt after you start the drag        |
| Double-click                 | Crop an image, a video, a camera, a gift or an emote                           |

## Limits

<details>
<summary>How much an overlay holds</summary>

| What              | Up to                               |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Elements          | 100 per overlay                     |
| Groups            | 100 per overlay                     |
| A text            | 500 characters                      |
| A name            | 60 characters                       |
| A text's **Size** | 400                                 |
| Guides            | 50 horizontal and 50 vertical       |
| Zoom              | 10 to 400 %                         |
| **Undo**          | 100 steps, while the editor is open |

When an overlay is full, the **+** menu says so.

</details>
