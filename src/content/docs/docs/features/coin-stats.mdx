**Coin stats**, in **Tools**, keeps every LIVE you connect Tokio to: its coins and gifts, and everyone who sent them.

## Each LIVE

A LIVE joins the list when it ends, newest first, with its date, length and coins. The bar under it compares its coins with your best LIVE's.

Select one to see its **Coins**, **Gifts** and **Gifters**, the most coins first. They count from when Tokio connected until the end.

Only gifts count as coins: treasure chests and Super Fan subscriptions don't.

## All time

**All time** adds up every LIVE and lists your **Top gifters**.

The search next to **Top gifters**, or **Gifters** on a LIVE, finds a viewer by name or @handle among everyone, not just the 100 listed, with their place.

Overlays can show the top 10 gifters of the LIVE and of all time: see [Gifters](/docs/developers/events/#gifters) to build one.

## Filtering by date

Above the list, pick **Last 24 hours**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** or **Last 12 months**. The list shows the LIVEs in that time, and the first row adds up the coins and gifts sent in it, with its **Top gifters**. A LIVE that runs past the start or the end counts only the gifts sent inside the dates, to the minute. **All time** brings back every LIVE.

For other dates, pick **Custom dates**, then **On**, **After**, **Before** or **Between** and the days. Each day can take a time, to the minute, except with **On**. A day without a time counts whole: **After** and **Before** leave it out, and **Between** includes it.

## Deleting

- To delete a LIVE, select it and click the trash button next to its date. Its gifts come off **All time** too.
- To clear the history, select **All time** and click the trash button next to its title. A LIVE you're connected to keeps counting.

<details>
<summary>How LIVEs are saved</summary>

Each profile keeps its last 1,500 LIVEs on your computer. Past that, the oldest goes, and its gifts come off **All time** too. Tokio saves the LIVE in progress every 30 seconds, so a crash, or closing Tokio before the LIVE ends, loses at most the last 30 seconds.

If the connection drops for good and you connect again, the LIVE shows up as two.

</details>
