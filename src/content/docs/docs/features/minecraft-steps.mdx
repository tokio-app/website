Every step a Games [action](/docs/features/games/#actions) can run, and what it does in the game. Steps run in order, as you, where you stand.

## Spawn mob

1 to 100 of a mob, with an optional name. The list has every mob the game can summon, plus **Chicken Jockey**: a baby zombie riding a chicken, with the name on the zombie.

Several mobs come one at a time, a quarter of a second apart. So 100 take about 25 seconds, and the action's next runs and **Test** wait for them.

Mobs you spawn don't burn in daylight, and hostile ones come after you.

- On Java, the Tokio mod does both. For their first second they attack no one, so a crowd can't kill you the moment it appears.
- On Bedrock, mobs that would burn get fire resistance. Hostile mobs attack when they see you, as usual, and cave spiders spawn angry. Endermen, zombified piglins and spiders in daylight stay calm until you hit them.

## Give item

1 to 100 of an item, from every item the game can give. It goes into your inventory, or drops at your feet when that's full.

Check **replacing yours** and the ones you already have of that item go first, enchanted or not, so gifts don't fill your inventory.

On Java, armor, weapons, tools, shields, elytras and fishing rods have an **enchanted** box. Check it and the item comes with the best enchantments it can have, each at its highest level, listed under the step.

A netherite sword gets Sharpness V, Sweeping Edge III, Fire Aspect II, Knockback II, Looting III, Unbreaking III and Mending.

<details>
<summary>Which enchantments</summary>

- Where two can't go together, the item gets Protection (not Fire, Blast or Projectile Protection), Sharpness (not Smite or Bane of Arthropods), Density (not Breach), Fortune (not Silk Touch), Depth Strider (not Frost Walker), Multishot (not Piercing), and Loyalty and Channeling (not Riptide).
- A bow gets Infinity, which can't go with Mending. It still needs an arrow in your inventory to shoot, so you may want to give one with it.
- No item gets a curse.
- **Replacing yours** on armor takes the piece you wear too, and the new one goes to your inventory.
- Bedrock actions have no **enchanted** box: their items come plain.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The finer points</summary>

- Bedrock's TNT has only two fuses: 4 s, or a random 0.5 to 2 s when you set 2 s or less.
- The anvil needs air 20 blocks up, and it stays in the world after it lands.
- A launch takes the seconds you set: fewer is faster. Above about 190 blocks, 1 second is too fast, so it takes a little longer.
- A launch first clears a 3×3 shaft as high as it goes, bedrock too, so in the Nether you go through the roof. The blocks it takes drop nothing. Near the top of the world, the part above it can't be cleared, and **Test** shows it failed.
- The cobweb trap only fills air, so it breaks nothing. The honey and obsidian boxes replace the blocks where their walls, floor and roof go, and leave what's inside.
- To get off the lava lake's island, jump the ring of lava, 2 blocks wide, or build your way out. Fall in and you catch fire.
- The hole never breaks the bedrock at the bottom of the world: that close to it, it digs nothing. In the End, a deep enough hole goes through the island.
- A gift streak gets a new random boss for every gift.
- In a LAN world, an Evoker's vexes and a Ravager's roar can reach other players too.
- A meteor lands on the ground under you even when you fly, and you have 2 seconds to get out of the way.
- A super laser's night only shows on your screen: the world's time doesn't change.
- The meteors' and lasers' particles follow your **Particles** setting in Minecraft: fewer on **Decreased**, none on **Minimal**.

</details>

## Help

- **Netherite armor**: a whole set, put on at once. The armor you wear is lost. On Java, check **enchanted** and each piece gets the best enchantments it can have, as with **Give item**.
- **Netherite kit**: the same armor, plus a netherite sword, pickaxe and axe, a bow and 64 arrows. On Java it all comes with the best enchantments, the bow with Infinity. Check **replacing yours** so more kits don't fill your inventory.
- **Full heal**: fills your health and hunger.
- **Regeneration** II, **Absorption** IV, **Resistance** IV, **Fire resistance**, **Speed** II, **Jump boost** II, **Strength** II, **Slow falling**, **Water breathing**, **Night vision** and **Invisibility**: for 1 to 600 seconds (30 to start).

## Turn into mob

Java only, through the Tokio mod. You become any living mob, bosses too, with its size, health, damage, armor and jump, and you see yourself as the mob in third person.

Under **How long**, pick **until turned back**, which a **Back to normal** step ends, or **for** 1 to 600 seconds (30 to start).

- Closing the world turns you back too.
- If you die, you respawn as the mob for the time you had left: its time stops while you're dead.
- While a timed one runs, a new **Turn into mob** waits until its time is up, then starts with its own seconds. They go in the order they came.
- A mob without seconds gives way to the next one at once, and **Back to normal** also drops the ones waiting.
- You keep the same share of your hearts, so half your health as a player is half an iron golem's 100.

While you're the mob:

- You walk at its pace, from half to one and a half times your usual walk.
- Flying mobs take off at once and fly as in creative mode: ghasts and blazes slower, phantoms, vexes and the Ender Dragon faster.
- Water mobs breathe underwater, and fish drown on land.
- Fireproof mobs don't burn, zombies and skeletons catch fire in the sun, endermen and blazes get hurt by water, and spiders climb walls.
- You make the mob's hurt, death, step and idle sounds.
- The Ender Dragon smashes through what it flies into, unless the `mobGriefing` game rule is off.

Guests in your LAN world need the mod to see the mob.

### Attacks

Press **R** to use the mob's own attack where you look, and hold it to keep attacking at the mob's pace. The key is **Mob attack**, under **Tokio** in **Options → Controls → Key Binds**.

Mobs that only bite or punch, like zombies and spiders, have nothing on R. They hit with left-click, for the mob's damage.

| Mob                                                              | What R does                                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Wither**                                                       | A wither skull from each of its heads in turn, one every 0.75 seconds                                                                                             |
| **Ghast**                                                        | Opens its mouth for a second, then fires a fireball that explodes, every 3 seconds                                                                                |
| **Blaze**                                                        | Three fireballs in a row, every 5.6 seconds                                                                                                                       |
| **Skeleton**, **Stray**, **Bogged**, **Parched**, **Illusioner** | An arrow every second, every 2.5 for the Bogged. A Stray's slows, a Bogged's poisons, a Parched's weakens                                                         |
| **Pillager**, **Piglin**                                         | A crossbow arrow every 2 seconds                                                                                                                                  |
| **Drowned**                                                      | A trident every 2 seconds                                                                                                                                         |
| **Witch**                                                        | A splash potion every 3 seconds: what a witch would throw at the mob you look at, or Harming                                                                      |
| **Snow Golem**                                                   | A snowball every second                                                                                                                                           |
| **Llama**, **Trader Llama**                                      | Spit every 2 seconds                                                                                                                                              |
| **Shulker**                                                      | A bullet that follows the mob you look at and makes it float, every 2 seconds                                                                                     |
| **Breeze**                                                       | Breathes in, then fires a wind charge, about every 1.5 seconds                                                                                                    |
| **Ender Dragon**                                                 | A dragon fireball that leaves a cloud of breath, every 3 seconds                                                                                                  |
| **Warden**                                                       | Charges for 1.7 seconds, then a sonic boom that goes through walls to the mob you aimed at, up to 15 blocks, every 5 seconds                                      |
| **Evoker**                                                       | Raises its arms, and a second later 16 fangs bite up in a line toward the mob you aimed at, or in two rings around you when it's within 3 blocks, every 5 seconds |
| **Guardian**, **Elder Guardian**                                 | A beam on the mob you look at, which hits after 4 seconds (3 for an Elder Guardian) if you keep it in sight                                                       |
| **Creeper**                                                      | Hisses for 1.5 seconds, then explodes around you, every 3 seconds. You live on as a creeper                                                                       |

Your left-click hits also do what the mob's own hit does: a Wither Skeleton's gives Wither, a Cave Spider's and a Bee's give Poison (not on Easy), an Iron Golem's tosses mobs up, and a Hoglin's and a Zoglin's throw them. Bare-handed, a Husk's makes mobs hungry, and a burning zombie's may set them on fire.

Your attacks never hurt you or other players, and the arrows and tridents you fire can't be picked up. Their blasts break blocks unless the `mobGriefing` game rule is off, and a Blaze's fireballs start fires.

## Command

Use a command step for anything else, like titles or sounds:

```
say {username} sent {count} {gift}!
```

Write it for the action's edition, since some commands differ. A mob or item that doesn't exist runs nothing.

It can use the action's [variables](/docs/features/games/#variables).
