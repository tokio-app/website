**Test voice** uses your replacements, **Remove emojis** and banned words, but not the format, the length limit or the permissions.

## Voices

| Engine         | Voices                                                                                                                                               |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **TikTok**     | TikTok's voices. Without your TikTok session ID in **Settings → Voice engines**, they go through `ottsy.weilbyte.dev`, a proxy run by a third party. |
| **Edge**       | Microsoft Edge's voices.                                                                                                                             |
| **Windows**    | The voices installed on Windows, SAPI and OneCore, 32-bit ones included. Only on Windows.                                                            |
| **ElevenLabs** | Your account's voices, clones included, in all of ElevenLabs' languages.                                                                             |
| **Fish Audio** | Your account's voices, clones included, and the library voices Fish Audio licensed from their owners, in all of its languages.                       |
| **Cartesia**   | Cartesia's voices and your clones, each in the language it was made for.                                                                             |
| **Inworld**    | Inworld's voices and your clones. Its key is the Base64 value from Inworld's Portal.                                                                 |

ElevenLabs, Fish Audio, Cartesia and Inworld need your own API key, added with **+** in **Settings → Voice engines**. Each message they read is paid from your account, and the service gets its text, as TikTok's proxy does.

## Clone your voice

Have chat read in your own voice, cloned with ElevenLabs, Fish Audio, Cartesia or Inworld.

<Steps>

1. Create an API key with one of them, and add it with **+** in **Settings → Voice engines**. The table below says what each one needs.

   <Screenshot
     name="settings-voice-engines"
     alt="Settings → Voice engines, with TikTok's session ID and the + menu listing ElevenLabs, Fish Audio, Cartesia and Inworld"
   />

2. On the **TTS** page, click **Clone a voice**, the microphone next to **Voice**. It shows once a key is set, and with more than one you pick the **Engine** first. Name the voice and add audio files, or record with **Record**.

   <Screenshot
     name="tts-clone"
     alt="Clone a voice with ElevenLabs: the voice's name, one sample and the permission checked"
   />

3. Check that the voice is yours or that you have the speaker's permission, then click **Clone voice**.
4. Give the new voice to a [special user](#special-users), and keep a free voice for the rest of chat. You pay for every message it reads.

</Steps>

| Engine         | To clone, you need                                                                       | Best samples     |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| **ElevenLabs** | A paid plan, and a key with access to Text to Speech and read and write access to Voices | 1 to 2 minutes   |
| **Fish Audio** | Balance in your account, which is pay as you go                                          | 10 to 60 seconds |
| **Cartesia**   | The Pro plan or a higher one                                                             | 10 to 60 seconds |
| **Inworld**    | A key with write access                                                                  | 10 to 30 seconds |

Samples work best with one person speaking clearly, without music or background noise.

<details>
<summary>Costs, limits and deleting a voice</summary>

Every message a voice from these services reads is paid from your account with it. On a busy LIVE, reading all of chat with ElevenLabs can spend 30,000 credits in an hour or less, while one special user's messages last many LIVEs. To cap what a LIVE can spend there, set a credit limit on the API key in ElevenLabs. When your credits or balance run out, Tokio tells you, and the voice stays quiet until you add more or pick another one. Meanwhile, Tokio tries that service again once a minute, or right away when you change its key.

A voice takes up to 10 samples of up to 25 MB each: MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, FLAC or WebM files. Tokio sends them only to the service you clone with and doesn't keep them. To rename or delete a cloned voice, use that service's website.

A recording stops by itself at the longest length in the table. Cartesia and Inworld learn from a single clip, so Tokio joins your samples into one, up to that length.

</details>

## Format

Pick which messages get read: **All messages**, **Messages starting with a period ( . )**, **Messages starting with a slash ( / )**, or a **Custom command** of your own, like `!tts`. The period, slash or command isn't read, and a blank command reads nothing.

<Screenshot
  name="tts-format"
  alt="The Format section: which messages get read, the message template, Remove emojis and Read translations"
/>

The **Message template** sets what's read:

| Placeholder     | Replaced with                                                                                           |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `{username}`    | The viewer's display name                                                                               |
| `{handle}`      | The viewer's handle, without the `@`                                                                    |
| `{comment}`     | The chat message                                                                                        |
| `{translation}` | The message's [translation](/docs/features/chat-translation/), or the message as typed when it has none |

`{username} {comment}` reads "Lucia hi from Lima!". **Remove emojis** leaves emojis out, and skips messages that are only emojis.

**Read translations** reads the [translation](/docs/features/chat-translation/#turn-it-on) in place of `{comment}`.

## Replacements

Teach the voice to say acronyms, slang and names. Each one swaps a whole word, in any case, before emojis are removed, so ❤️ can become "love".

## Permissions

Uncheck **Allow everyone**, then check who can talk. Anyone with one of the checked roles gets through, and with none checked, everyone does. Each platform has its own tab:

| Platform             | Rules                                                                   |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **TikTok**           | **Followers**, **Super Fans**, **Moderators** and **Minimum fan level** |
| **Twitch**, **Kick** | **Subscribers**, **Moderators** and **VIPs**                            |
| **YouTube**          | **Members** and **Moderators**                                          |

**Moderators** includes you, and **Subscribers** includes founders.

With **Use allow and block lists**, an allow list with names lets only them talk, and @handles on the block list are never read. Both lists apply on every platform. On Kick, add a viewer by the name in their kick.com link: `shark-34` for Shark_34.

## Protection

The **Cooldown** is the seconds between two messages, for all of chat. Messages that come while the queue holds **Max queue length** messages are dropped.

Messages over **Max message length**, or with a banned word in the comment, name or @handle, are skipped, even with look-alike letters. **Banned words** also match inside other words, so banning "ass" skips "class" too.

<Screenshot
  name="tts-protection"
  alt="The Protection section: cooldown, maximum queue length, maximum comment length and banned words"
/>

<details>
<summary>Messages that are skipped</summary>

- A message whose filled template has no letter, number or emoji, like `{comment}` with "?" alone.
- A message whose [translation](/docs/features/chat-translation/) is over **Max message length**.
- A message with a banned word written with hidden characters in it.

</details>

[Alerts that speak](/docs/features/alerts/#spoken-messages) use the replacements, **Remove emojis**, banned words and the block list too, and [music](/docs/features/music-requests/#who-can-request) has its own permissions.
