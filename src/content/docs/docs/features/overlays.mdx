Overlays are web pages on your stream that react to your LIVE, like a gift jar or a likes leaderboard.

## Install an official overlay

On **Overlays**, click **Official overlays**, then **Install** on the one you want. It shows in the gallery marked **Verified**.

<Screenshot
  name="overlays-official"
  alt="An official overlay's card in Official overlays, with its preview and the Install button"
/>

When a new version is out, a dot shows on **Official overlays**: click **Update**. Your settings are kept where they still fit; refresh the overlay in your streaming app afterwards.

An official overlay's address ends in its own name, like `/gift-jar/`, so it stays the same if you remove and install it again.

## Import your own

Import an overlay's folder in **Settings → Overlay library**. It shows marked **External** and gets no updates. See [Building an overlay](/docs/developers/building-an-overlay/) for what the folder needs.

Tokio keeps a copy, so later edits to the folder don't reach it. Importing the folder again makes a second overlay, with a new address and default settings.

<Screenshot
  name="settings-overlay-library"
  alt="The Overlay library in Settings, with one imported overlay"
/>

## Set it up

Click **Customize** to change an overlay's colors, text or gifts; it shows only on overlays with settings. The preview shows your changes, and **Save** sends them to your stream.

The **Test** in **Customize** stays in its preview. Overlays don't follow Tokio's language.

<Screenshot
  name="overlays-customize"
  alt="The Gift Jar's settings, with its preview next to them"
/>

## Add it to your stream

Copy the address from the overlay's card. Its **Test** sends sample events to the preview and to the overlay on your stream. See [Adding an overlay](/docs/guides/adding-an-overlay/) for the rest.

<Screenshot
  name="overlays-card"
  alt="An installed overlay in the gallery, with Test, Customize, its preview and its URL"
/>

## Remove an overlay

Use the trash button on its card in **Official overlays**, or in **Settings → Overlay library** for one you imported. It's deleted with its settings, after you confirm.

## Make your own

Build one in the app with [Overlay Studio](/docs/features/overlay-studio/), from text, images and the overlays you have; the ones you build live there. Or write one by hand or with any framework: see [Building an overlay](/docs/developers/building-an-overlay/).
