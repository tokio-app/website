Overlays are web pages on your stream that react to your LIVE, like a gift jar or a likes leaderboard.

<Screenshot
  name="overlays-gallery"
  alt="Two installed overlays on the Overlays page, Gift Jar and Top Likes, each marked Verified with Test and Customize, a preview and its address"
/>

## Install an official overlay

On **Overlays**, click **Official overlays**, then **Install** on the one you want. It shows in the gallery marked **Verified**.

<Screenshot
  name="overlays-official"
  alt="An official overlay's card in Official overlays, with its preview and the Install button"
/>

When a new version is out, a dot shows on **Official overlays**: click **Update**. Your settings are kept where they still fit; refresh the overlay in your streaming app afterwards.

## Import your own

Import an overlay's folder in **Settings → Overlay library**. It shows marked **External** and gets no updates.

<Screenshot
  name="settings-overlay-library"
  alt="The Overlay library in Settings, with one imported overlay"
/>

## Set it up

Click **Customize** to change an overlay's colors, text or gifts. The preview shows your changes, and **Save** sends them to your stream.

<Screenshot
  name="overlays-customize"
  alt="The Gift Jar's settings, with its preview next to them"
/>

## Add it to your stream

Copy the address from the overlay's card; **Test** sends it sample events. See [Adding an overlay](/docs/guides/adding-an-overlay/) for the rest.

<Screenshot
  name="overlays-card"
  alt="An installed overlay in the gallery, with Test, Customize, its preview and its URL"
/>

## Remove an overlay

Use the trash button on its card in **Official overlays**, or in **Settings → Overlay library** for one you imported. It's deleted with its settings, after you confirm.

## Make your own

Write one by hand or with any framework: see [Building an overlay](/docs/developers/building-an-overlay/).
