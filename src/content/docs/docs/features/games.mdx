## Connect Minecraft

Tokio works with Bedrock Edition by itself, and with Java Edition through the Tokio mod. Both connect from the game's chat, one game at a time.

On **Games**, turn on **Minecraft**: the status reads **Waiting for Minecraft**. Tokio listens only while it's on, and the switch is the same for all profiles.

## Bedrock Edition

Follow the steps **Games** shows for Bedrock, in Minecraft:

<Screenshot
  name="games-connect-bedrock"
  alt="The Bedrock Edition steps on Games: turn on Enable WebSockets in Settings → General → Network settings, open a world with cheats on, and paste the command in the chat, here /connect 192.168.1.20:37892"
/>

**Require Encrypted WebSockets** can stay on. Leaving the world ends the connection, so type the `/connect` command again next time.

<details>
<summary>If it doesn't connect</summary>

- **Port 37892 is in use**: another program holds the port. Close it, then turn **Minecraft** off and on.
- The game says WebSockets are off: turn on **Enable WebSockets**.
- The game won't take the command: the world needs cheats on.

</details>

## Java Edition

The Tokio mod, for Fabric, connects single-player and LAN worlds you host, not dedicated servers. Cheats can stay off: it runs commands as you, with a command block's permission, and their answers don't show in chat.

In a LAN world, Tokio's mobs never go after other players, and its explosions, lightning, arrows, potions and dragon's breath don't hurt them. Only you see the meteors and lasers.

### Install the mod

You do this once, for Minecraft Java **26.3**.

<Steps>

1. Install [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/installer/) for 26.3.
2. Put [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) for 26.3 in Minecraft's `mods` folder.
3. Download <ModDownload /> and put it in the same folder.
4. Start Minecraft with the Fabric profile.

</Steps>

### Connect

Open a world, then type the command **Games** shows in the game's chat:

<Screenshot
  name="games-connect-java"
  alt="The Java Edition steps on Games: install Fabric Loader, Fabric API and the Tokio mod for 26.3, open a world, and paste the command in the chat, here /tokio connect 192.168.1.20:37892"
/>

On the same PC as Tokio, `/tokio connect` is enough. The mod never reconnects by itself: connect each time you open the world.

| Command                    | What it does                               |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| `/tokio`                   | Says whether the world is connected        |
| `/tokio connect [address]` | Connects, to `localhost:37892` without one |
| `/tokio disconnect`        | Disconnects from Tokio                     |

Only the world's host can use them.

<details>
<summary>If it doesn't connect</summary>

- The game doesn't know `/tokio`: check the Fabric profile and the `mods` folder.
- **Couldn't connect to Tokio at …**: turn on **Minecraft**, and use an address from **Games**.
- **Another game connected to Tokio.**: another game replaced this one.
- **Minecraft was turned off in Tokio.**: the switch on **Games** was turned off.

</details>

## Another device

The same command works from a phone or another PC on your network. With more than one address, **Games** shows a command for each: use the one on your device's network.

## Actions

An action is a trigger and the steps it runs. Actions belong to the profile, and each edition has its own tab: **Bedrock** and **Java**.

<Steps>

1. Pick the tab of your game's edition.
2. Click **Add action** under its actions. It starts as a Follow that spawns one Zombie.
3. Pick the trigger, change the step, and click **Add step** for more.
4. Click the play button to test it.

</Steps>

<Screenshot
  name="games-actions"
  alt="The Bedrock tab of Actions: a follow that spawns a zombie named after the viewer, and a gift that runs a say command"
/>

Each action has its own on/off switch. **Remove action** and each step's **Remove step** delete at once, without asking.

**Games** opens on the connected game's tab, or on **Java** when only Java has actions, and turns to a game's tab when it connects. A new action takes its tab's edition.

**Duplicate action** puts a copy right after the action: change its edition to run the same steps in the other game.

<details>
<summary>Changing an action's edition</summary>

- The action moves to the other tab.
- Its mobs and items become the other edition's, or a zombie or a diamond when that edition has none like them.
- On Bedrock, **Meteor** becomes **Falling TNT**, the lasers become **Lightning bolt**, and **Turn into mob** and **Back to normal** go away.
- Runs still waiting for its old edition are dropped.

</details>

Click **Actions** to fold the actions away and give the overlay more room. They open again the next time you open **Games**.

Triggers work as for [alerts](/docs/features/alerts/#triggers), without joins, envelopes, Super Fan Boxes and song changes. They have no **Worth at least** or **From**.

| Trigger   | Options                                                                                                               |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Gift**  | The gifts, and **When streak ends** or **Every gift**, as in [gift alerts](/docs/features/alerts/#gift-alerts)        |
| **Chat**  | A keyword or phrase the message must have                                                                             |
| **Like**  | Every how many likes, 100 to start. All viewers' likes count together, and the count starts over when you disconnect. |
| **Emote** | The emotes, or any                                                                                                    |

Actions run while **Minecraft** is on and a game of their edition is connected, whatever's on at **Home**.

## Steps

Steps run in order, as you, where you stand. [Minecraft steps](/docs/features/minecraft-steps/) has what each one does in the game.

| Step                                                                | What it does                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [**Spawn mob**](/docs/features/minecraft-steps/#spawn-mob)          | 1 to 100 of a mob, with an optional name                                         |
| [**Turn into mob**](/docs/features/minecraft-steps/#turn-into-mob)  | Java only: you become a mob, until **Back to normal** or for the seconds you set |
| [**Back to normal**](/docs/features/minecraft-steps/#turn-into-mob) | Java only: ends **Turn into mob**                                                |
| [**Give item**](/docs/features/minecraft-steps/#give-item)          | 1 to 100 of an item, to you                                                      |
| **Weather**                                                         | **Clear**, **Rain** or **Thunder**                                               |
| **Time of day**                                                     | **Day**, **Noon**, **Night** or **Midnight**                                     |
| [**Hazard**](/docs/features/minecraft-steps/#hazards)               | Something that tries to kill you or gets in your way                             |
| [**Help**](/docs/features/minecraft-steps/#help)                    | Something that helps you survive                                                 |
| [**Command**](/docs/features/minecraft-steps/#command)              | Any command, as you'd type it in chat, slash or not                              |

## Variables

A mob's name and a command can use these. Click one to add it.

| Variable                 | Triggers                                          |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------- |
| `{username}`, `{handle}` | All but Like                                      |
| `{comment}`              | Chat, Emote                                       |
| `{gift}`                 | Gift                                              |
| `{count}`                | Gift (the streak's size, not with **Every gift**) |

Viewers pick their own names and messages, so Tokio cleans what a variable adds to a command.

<details>
<summary>What it removes</summary>

Quotes (`"` and `'`), backslashes, brackets and braces (`[ ] { }`), `§`, `@` and control characters. It also cuts the text to 100 characters. Your own text in the step stays as you wrote it.

</details>

## Show your actions on stream

The **Overlay** section shows viewers what each event does, with a picture and name you pick.

<Steps>

1. Turn on the overlay server in **Settings → Overlay server**.
2. Copy the address in **Overlay URL**.
3. Add it to your streaming app, as in [Adding an overlay](/docs/guides/adding-an-overlay/).

</Steps>

<Screenshot
  name="games-overlay"
  alt="The Overlay section: its URL, a row per action, the preview and the look options"
/>

```
http://tokio-app.local:37891/games/minecraft/
```

Split it into up to 4 **Columns**, or **Rows** when it's horizontal, to spread it over the screen. Each gets its own address under **Overlay URLs**, like `http://tokio-app.local:37891/games/minecraft/2/`, and fills its source, shrinking to fit.

### Rows

Each action has a row, in the actions' order:

- **Show on the overlay** puts it on the overlay. It needs the action on, and a picture or a name.
- The event shows a gift's or emote's picture, or Tokio's own pixel icon. Type over its text to change it; empty it to go back.
- The square picks the picture.
- The name starts from the steps, like **Zombie**. Type your own, or empty it to go back.
- **Column** (**Row** when horizontal) picks where it shows, with more than one.
- Drag a row by the dots on its left to move it up or down, or reach them with Tab and use the arrow keys. **Actions** lists them in the same order.

### Pictures

Tokio can't include Minecraft's pictures, so you add your own: png, jpg, gif or webp, up to 50 MB. Click the square, then **Browse files...** to add one or several.

All profiles share the library, in the `games/minecraft` folder inside the [`.tokio` folder](/docs/features/profiles-and-settings/#your-files) in your user folder. **Clear** takes the picture off an action; its delete button in the picker takes it off every action and deletes the file.

| **Text**, **Accent**, **Arrow** | The colors of the texts, a lit action and the arrows                                                                                         |

### Light-up

When an action runs in the game, it lights up on the overlay in the accent color. A burst keeps it lit until 1.5 seconds after the last run.

The play button on **Overlay** lights up the next action, in the preview and on stream.

<details>
<summary>Limits</summary>

- Every gift on **Every gift** and every count on **Like** runs, however many come at once.
- Actions on other events keep at most 100 runs waiting. More are skipped until the game catches up, so a busy chat can't put them minutes behind.
- An action's runs go one at a time, and other actions don't wait for them. A big **Spawn mob** holds its action: 100 mobs take about 25 seconds.
- Turning an action off, removing it, changing its edition, turning **Minecraft** off or the game leaving stops its waiting runs. Disconnecting from the LIVE doesn't.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Notices</summary>

- **Minecraft Java Edition isn't connected, so the … action didn't run.** (or Bedrock Edition): an action ran with no game of its edition. At most once a minute.
- **A command of the … action failed in Minecraft:** then the game's answer. At most once every 30 seconds per action.
- **The … action has 100 runs waiting, so new ones are skipped until Minecraft catches up.** At most once every 30 seconds per action.
- **Tokio couldn't listen for Minecraft. Port 37892 may be in use.**: the port was in use when Tokio started.

</details>
