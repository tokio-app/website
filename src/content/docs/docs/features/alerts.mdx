Alerts play a sound or a message, or show an image or a video, when something happens in your LIVE.

<Screenshot
  name="alerts"
  alt="Five named alerts on the Alerts page: Thank followers speaks a message, Big gifts plays a cut sound and shows an image for two gifts, Hype answers a chat keyword, Shares plays for shares and Regulars for one viewer joining"
/>

Alerts belong to the profile; your sounds and media, in the [Library](#library), are shared by all profiles.

## Make an alert

<Steps>

1. On the **Alerts** page, click **Add alert**, open the new alert and type a **Name**.
2. On the **Trigger** tab, pick the event.

   <Screenshot
     name="alerts-trigger"
     alt="The Trigger tab of a gift alert for any gift worth at least 100 coins, from moderators"
   />

3. On the **Sound** tab, click **Choose sound...** and pick a sound.

   <Screenshot
     name="alerts-sounds"
     alt="The sound picker: your sounds, MyInstants and text-to-speech tabs, and Browse file"
   />

4. On the **Image or video** tab, add an image or a video, if you like.
5. Turn on **Alerts** on **Home**, and test the alert with its play button.

</Steps>

Each alert's row has its own on/off switch, and **Home** counts how many are on.

## Triggers

Pick the **Event**. To play one alert on more than one, click **Add a trigger**: up to five, each with its own filters.

| Platform    | Events                                                                                                     | **From**                                      |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| **TikTok**  | Chat, Emote, Follow, Share, Like, Gift, Envelope, Super Fan, Super Fan Box, Super Fan join and Member join | **Followers**, **Moderators**, **Super Fans** |
| **Twitch**  | Chat, Emote, Follow, Subscription, Gifted subs, Bits, Raid and Channel points                              | **Moderators**, **Subscribers**, **VIPs**     |
| **YouTube** | Chat, Emote, Gift, Subscription, Gifted subs and Super Chat                                                | **Moderators**, **Members**                   |
| **Kick**    | Chat, Emote, Kicks, Subscription, Gifted subs, Raid and Channel points                                     | **Moderators**, **Subscribers**, **VIPs**     |

**Music change** comes from Tokio, for each new song from [music requests](/docs/features/music-requests/). A Like alert plays for every batch of likes TikTok sends.

| Trigger             | Plays when                                                                             |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Subscription**    | A viewer subscribes or renews, or becomes a YouTube member or reaches a milestone      |
| **Gifted subs**     | A viewer gifts subs or memberships, once for the whole gift                            |
| **Bits**, **Kicks** | A viewer cheers with Bits on Twitch, or sends Kicks on Kick                            |
| **Super Chat**      | A viewer sends a Super Chat or a Super Sticker                                         |
| **Raid**            | Another channel raids you, or hosts you on Kick                                        |
| **Channel points**  | A viewer redeems a reward. Type its title to play only for it, or leave **Any reward** |

Twitch has Bits and channel points only on Affiliate and Partner channels.

**From** limits Chat, Emote and TikTok's gifts to the viewers you check. **Moderators** includes you, and **Subscribers** includes founders.

### Platforms

With [two or more platforms](/docs/connect/#several-platforms-together), each trigger starts with **Any platform** or one of yours. A trigger offers only what its platforms send: their events, emotes, roles and variables. **Music change** plays only on **Any platform**, and there **Followers** only passes TikTok's viewers.

### Minimums

Bits, Kicks, Super Chats, gifted subs and gifts on **Any gift** take a minimum. At 0, any amount plays.

| Trigger         | Minimum                                                                                                                       |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Gift**        | **Worth at least**, in coins on TikTok and Jewels on YouTube: a streak's worth when it ends, or each gift's on **Every gift** |
| **Bits**        | **Worth at least**, in Bits                                                                                                   |
| **Kicks**       | **Worth at least**, in Kicks                                                                                                  |
| **Super Chat**  | **Worth at least**, in US dollars (USD). One whose currency Tokio has no rate for always plays                                |
| **Gifted subs** | **At least**, in subs gifted at once                                                                                          |

[Overlay Studio's reactions](/docs/features/overlay-studio/#when) take the same minimums.

## Gift alerts

On the **Trigger** tab, click **Any gift** to pick TikTok's gifts. **Select all shown** checks every gift the filters leave.

<Screenshot
  name="alerts-gifts"
  alt="The gift list filtered to gifts of at least 1,000 coins, with Galaxy checked"
/>

Then choose when it plays:

- **When streak ends**: once per combo, so a streak of 20 roses plays it once. The default.
- **Every gift**: once per gift, up to the queue's length.

<details>
<summary>YouTube's Gifts, and gifts missing from the list</summary>

YouTube's Gifts come whole, a combo as one event, so a YouTube trigger has no choice of when it plays. They aren't in the gift list, so they play on **Any gift**.

Their minimum is in Jewels. On **Any platform**, **Worth at least** counts coins for TikTok's gifts and Jewels for YouTube's.

TikTok's list misses some gifts, like other regions' ones. Tokio adds the ones viewers send.

</details>

## Chat, emote and join alerts

- **Chat**: type a keyword or phrase to play only for messages with it as a whole word. **hi** plays for "Hi there!" and "hi❤️everyone", but not for "this".
- **Emote**: click **Any emote** to pick emotes. The list fills as viewers use them. It keeps the 300 most recent on each platform, and never drops an emote you picked.
- **Member join** and **Super Fan join**: type an @handle or name for an entrance sound for one viewer.

## Sounds

Pick one of your sounds, search [MyInstants](https://www.myinstants.com), or **Browse file...** for an mp3, wav or ogg up to 8 MB. **Clear** takes it off the alert, and **Volume** sets how loud it plays.

## Cutting a sound

Drag the waveform's handles to cut the start or the end, or type the seconds in **Start** and **End**. The play button next to **Reset cut** plays only the cut.

<Screenshot
  name="alerts-cut"
  alt="The Sound tab: a waveform cut from 0.2 to 1.3 seconds of a 2 second sound"
/>

<details>
<summary>More on cuts</summary>

- With a handle focused, the arrow keys move it by a tenth of a second, or a second with Shift.
- The file stays whole, so other alerts can cut it their own way.
- The alert's row shows the cut's length, and its play button plays only the cut.

</details>

## Spoken messages

On the sound picker's **Text-to-speech** tab, type a message, pick a voice, and press **Use text-to-speech**. Its **Speed** and **Pitch** start from the TTS page's.

<Screenshot
  name="alerts-tts"
  alt="The Text-to-speech tab of the sound picker, with a message that thanks the viewer for the follow"
/>

Click a variable to add the event's details. The editor offers only the ones your trigger's platforms fill.

| Variable                 | Triggers                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `{username}`, `{handle}` | All but Envelope, Super Fan Box and Music change                                                                                                                                                                   |
| `{comment}`              | Chat and Emote; Subscription (not on Kick), Bits, Kicks and Super Chat (what the viewer wrote); Channel points (what they typed)                                                                                   |
| `{translation}`          | Chat and Emote: the message's [translation](/docs/features/chat-translation/), or the message as typed                                                                                                             |
| `{gift}`                 | Gift                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| `{count}`                | Gift (the combo size, not on **Every gift** or YouTube), Like (the likes sent), Subscription (the months so far), Gifted subs (the subs gifted), Bits and Kicks (the amount sent) and Raid (the viewers it brings) |
| `{reward}`               | Channel points (the reward's title)                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `{amount}`               | Super Chat (what it cost, as YouTube shows it, like $4.99)                                                                                                                                                         |
| `{song}`                 | Music change                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

The play button fills them with a sample viewer.

The [TTS page's](/docs/features/text-to-speech/) replacements, **Remove emojis**, banned words, cooldown and queue length apply too. A banned word in a name or comment skips the whole alert, image or video included. For a viewer on the block list, only the message is left out.

## Images and videos

Click **Choose a file...** for a png, jpg, gif, webp, mp4 or webm up to 50 MB. An image stays for its **Duration**, up to 60 seconds, or longer if the sound is longer.

<Screenshot
  name="alerts-media"
  alt="The Image or video tab: the alert's image, its duration and the alert's overlay URL"
/>

Each alert shows its media on its own overlay:

```
http://tokio-app.local:37891/alerts/<alert-id>/
```

Copy it from the alert's row, [add it to your streaming app](/docs/guides/adding-an-overlay/), and press the alert's play button to check it.

<details>
<summary>Videos</summary>

- A video stays while it plays, up to 60 seconds.
- **Cut** keeps part of it, like a sound's cut.
- A video that doesn't say its length can't be cut: set its **Duration** instead.
- A video plays its own sound, at its own **Volume**.
- To hear videos in a browser tab, see [Fonts and sound](/docs/guides/adding-an-overlay/#fonts-and-sound).

</details>

## Library

**Library**, in the header of the **Alerts** page, holds your sounds, images and videos. Click **+** to add many at once.

<Screenshot
  name="alerts-library"
  alt="The Library's Sounds tab with four sounds and their lengths, next to the Images and videos tab"
/>

Deleting a file removes it from every alert, after you confirm, and empties the Overlay Studio elements that showed it.

## Queue

Alerts play one at a time. **Max queue length** drops the extras, and **Play alerts at the same time** lets different triggers overlap.

<Screenshot
  name="alerts-queue"
  alt="The Alerts page header, with Play alerts at the same time and Max queue length"
/>

An alert with media holds the queue for its duration, so keep it short for likes. Alerts stop when you disconnect.
