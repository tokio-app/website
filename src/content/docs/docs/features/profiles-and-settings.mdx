Profiles keep each stream's setup apart, and **Settings** holds what they all share. Tokio saves your changes as you make them.

## Profiles

Each profile has its own TTS, music, alerts, game actions, countdown, [Coin stats](/docs/features/coin-stats/) and [fans](/docs/features/fans/), and remembers the username you last connected with. Switch profiles from the title bar, or under **Active profile** in **Settings → Live profiles**, while you're not connected.

<Screenshot
  name="profiles-menu"
  alt="The profile menu in the title bar, with two profiles"
/>

Every profile shares the installed overlays, Overlay Studio's overlays, the libraries, the test overlays, Home's **Active features** and **Activity feed**, and the Minecraft connection.

In **Settings → Live profiles**, **New** creates a blank profile and **Clone** copies one you have, without its Coin stats or fans. Names need 3 or more characters and must be unique, and profiles can't be renamed or deleted.

<Screenshot
  name="settings-profiles"
  alt="Settings → Live profiles, with the active profile and the New, Clone, Import and Export buttons"
/>

**Export** saves the active profile's settings as a file, and **Import** adds one as a new profile. The file holds no sounds, images, videos, game pictures, Coin stats or fans, so on another computer its alerts report a missing sound until you pick it again.

## Settings

| Section            | What it's for                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **TikTok LIVE**    | Your [Euler Stream](https://www.eulerstream.com) API key, which Tokio needs to connect. [Sign up](https://www.eulerstream.com/register) for free.                                                                                                                                     |
| **Overlay server** | Turns on the server for overlays, and shows your network address for a streaming app on [another computer](/docs/guides/adding-an-overlay/#on-another-computer).                                                                                                                      |
| **Language**       | English or Spanish. **System language** follows your computer's, falling back to English. It changes Tokio's menus and messages, and the text Tokio writes on the games and timer overlays, but not the TTS voices.                                                                   |
| **Updates**        | Turns off the automatic checks for new versions.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Tray**           | Keeps Tokio in the tray (the menu bar on macOS) when you close it. Click the icon or **Open Tokio** to bring it back, and **Quit Tokio** to quit. On Linux, the first time you close it after Tokio starts, a notification says Tokio is still running: click it to bring Tokio back. |
| **Voice engines**  | Your TikTok session ID, so TikTok voices come straight from TikTok instead of a third-party proxy.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Logs**           | **Export logs**, the button next to the section's title, saves all of Tokio's logs in one .zip to attach to a bug report. **Detailed logs** also writes every LIVE event to them, after you confirm. They grow faster that way, so turn it off once you've sent them.                 |

<Screenshot
  name="settings"

Opening Tokio while it's already running brings its window to the front. **About**, in the sidebar, shows Tokio's version, its license and the third-party notices, and has **Check for updates** and **Take the tour**.

## Libraries

**Sound library** and **Media library** hold the sounds, images and videos your alerts use, in every profile. Upload and preview them there.

<Screenshot
  name="settings-libraries"
  alt="The Sound library with four sounds and the Media library with one image"
/>

Deleting a sound removes it from every alert, after you confirm. Deleting an image or video also empties the Overlay Studio elements that showed it.

**Overlay library** holds the [overlays you import](/docs/features/overlays/#import-your-own).

## Notifications

When something fails, such as a voice that can't be made or a missing sound, Tokio tells you what went wrong. The bell in the title bar keeps the session's last 50, and **Clear all** empties it.

## Your files

Tokio keeps everything in the `.tokio` folder in your user folder: `C:\Users\<you>\.tokio` on Windows, `~/.tokio` on macOS and Linux.

| Folder            | What's in it                              |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| `settings`        | Settings, and what every profile shares   |
| `profiles`        | Each profile's settings                   |
| `sounds`, `media` | The Sound library and Media library       |
| `overlays`        | The overlays you installed or imported    |
| `games/minecraft` | The Minecraft pictures                    |
| `fans`            | Each profile's fans                       |
| `history`         | Each profile's past LIVEs, for Coin stats |
| `logs`            | What Tokio did, for bug reports           |

To report a bug, open an [issue on GitHub](https://github.com/tokio-app/tokio/issues) and attach the .zip that **Export logs** saves in **Settings → Logs**. The logs include your TikTok username, and with **Detailed logs** on, your viewers' usernames too.
