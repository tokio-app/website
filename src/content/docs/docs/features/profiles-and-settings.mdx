Profiles keep each stream's setup apart, and **Settings** holds what they all share. Tokio saves your changes as you make them.

## Profiles

Each profile has its own TTS, music, alerts, game actions, countdown, [Coin stats](/docs/features/coin-stats/) and [fans](/docs/features/fans/), and remembers the username, YouTube channel and Kick channel you last connected with and the [platforms](/docs/connect/#several-platforms-together) it connects to. Switch profiles from the title bar, or under **Active profile** in **Settings → Live profiles**, while you're not connected.

<Screenshot
  name="profiles-menu"
  alt="The profile menu in the title bar, with two profiles"
/>

Every profile shares the installed overlays, Overlay Studio's overlays, the [Library](/docs/features/alerts/#library), the test overlays, Home's **Active features** and **Activity feed**, and the Minecraft connection.

In **Settings → Live profiles**, **New** creates a blank profile and **Clone** copies one you have, without its Coin stats or fans. Names need 3 or more characters and must be unique, and profiles can't be renamed or deleted.

<Screenshot
  name="settings-profiles"
  alt="Settings → Live profiles, with the active profile and the New, Clone, Import and Export buttons"
/>

**Export** saves the active profile's settings as a file, and **Import** adds one as a new profile. The file holds no sounds, images, videos, game pictures, Coin stats or fans, so on another computer its alerts report a missing sound until you pick it again.

## Settings

| Section              | What it's for                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **TikTok LIVE**      | Your [Euler Stream](https://www.eulerstream.com) API key, which Tokio needs to connect. [Sign up](https://www.eulerstream.com/register) for free.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Twitch**           | Signs Tokio in to your Twitch channel, with **Sign in with Twitch**, so it can connect to it. The account is shared by every profile. See [Sign in to Twitch](/docs/connect/#sign-in-to-twitch).                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Overlay server**   | Turns on the server for overlays, and shows your network address for a streaming app on [another computer](/docs/guides/adding-an-overlay/#on-another-computer).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Language**         | English or Spanish. **System language** follows your computer's, falling back to English. It changes Tokio's menus and messages, the text Tokio writes on the games and timer overlays, and an overlay's settings when the overlay translates them, but not the TTS voices.                                                                                                                           |
| **Chat translation** | Translates viewers' messages into the language you pick, for free with Google, and **Test** tries it. See [Chat translation](/docs/features/chat-translation/).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Updates**          | Turns off the automatic checks for new versions of Tokio, and the [automatic updates of official overlays](/docs/features/overlays/#install-an-official-overlay).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Tray**             | Keeps Tokio in the tray (the menu bar on macOS) when you close it. Click the icon or **Open Tokio** to bring it back, and **Quit Tokio** to quit. On Linux, the first time you close it after Tokio starts, a notification says Tokio is still running: click it to bring Tokio back.                                                                                                                 |
| **Shortcuts**        | Keys for the [live controls](/docs/features/live-controls/), which work in any app: skip a song, stop TTS, replay the last alert and pause everything.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Remote control**   | The [live controls](/docs/features/live-controls/#from-your-phone-or-another-computer) on your phone or another computer, through a QR code or its link, and the key for the [Control API](/docs/developers/control-api/) and [MCP](/docs/developers/mcp/). Off until you check it.                                                                                                                   |
| **Voice engines**    | Your TikTok session ID, so TikTok voices come straight from TikTok instead of a third-party proxy. Your API keys for ElevenLabs, Fish Audio, Cartesia or Inworld, for their voices and to [clone your own](/docs/features/text-to-speech/#clone-your-voice). Add one with the **+** next to **Voice engines**. Removing a key puts the default voice back wherever that service's voices were picked. |
| **Logs**             | **Export logs**, the button next to the section's title, saves all of Tokio's logs in one .zip to attach to a bug report. **Detailed logs** also writes every LIVE event to them, after you confirm. They grow faster that way, so turn it off once you've sent them.                                                                                                                                 |

Opening Tokio while it's already running brings its window to the front. **About**, in the sidebar, shows Tokio's version, its license and the third-party notices, and has **Check for updates** and **Take the tour**.

## Notifications

When something fails, such as a voice that can't be made or a missing sound, Tokio tells you what went wrong. The bell in the title bar keeps the session's last 50, and **Clear all** empties it.

## Your files

Tokio keeps your settings and files in the `.tokio` folder in your user folder: `C:\Users\<you>\.tokio` on Windows, `~/.tokio` on macOS and Linux.

| Folder            | What's in it                                                                              |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `settings`        | Settings, and what every profile shares                                                   |
| `profiles`        | Each profile's settings                                                                   |
| `sounds`, `media` | The Library's sounds, images and videos                                                   |
| `overlays`        | The overlays you installed or imported                                                    |
| `games/minecraft` | The Minecraft pictures                                                                    |
| `fans`            | Each profile's fans                                                                       |
| `history`         | Each profile's past LIVEs, for Coin stats                                                 |
| `extra`           | TikTok's gift list, the gifts viewers sent that aren't on it, and Block ads' filter lists |
| `logs`            | What Tokio did, for bug reports                                                           |

Your YouTube and Spotify sign-ins are in Tokio's app data folder instead: `%APPDATA%\tokio` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/tokio` on macOS and `~/.config/tokio` on Linux.

To report a bug, open an [issue on GitHub](https://github.com/tokio-app/tokio/issues) and attach the .zip that **Export logs** saves in **Settings → Logs**. The logs include your TikTok username, and with **Detailed logs** on, your viewers' usernames too.
