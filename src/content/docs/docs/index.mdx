Tokio is a desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux that reacts to your TikTok LIVE. It reads chat aloud, takes song requests, plays alerts, runs commands in Minecraft and drives your overlays.

## What you need

- A computer with Windows 10 or later, macOS 13 or later, or Linux, open while you stream.
- A free [Euler Stream](https://www.eulerstream.com/register) API key, to connect to your LIVE.
- For overlays, OBS Studio, TikTok LIVE Studio or another app with a browser source.
- For music, Spotify Premium, or YouTube. With YouTube Premium you skip the ads.
- For games, Minecraft Bedrock Edition, or Java Edition with Fabric and the [Tokio mod](/docs/features/games/#java-edition).

## Features

- **[Overlays](/docs/features/overlays/)**: install official overlays or import your own.
- **[Text-to-speech](/docs/features/text-to-speech/)**: chat read aloud.
- **[Alerts](/docs/features/alerts/)**: sounds, voices, images and videos for gifts, follows and more.
- **[Music](/docs/features/music-requests/)**: viewers queue songs on YouTube or Spotify.
- **[Games](/docs/features/games/)**: gifts and chat spawn mobs and run commands in Minecraft.
- **[Chat commands](/docs/features/chat-commands/)**: what viewers can type, like `!play`.
- **[Countdown](/docs/features/countdown-timer/)**: a clock viewers add time to.
- **[Overlay Studio](/docs/features/overlay-studio/)**: your own overlay, built from text, pictures, videos and cameras.
- **[Coin stats](/docs/features/coin-stats/)**: the coins and gifts of every LIVE and who sent them.
- **[Fans](/docs/features/fans/)**: the viewers with a fan level, highest first.
