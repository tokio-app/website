---
title: Connect to your LIVE
description: Connect Tokio to your TikTok LIVE and turn on its features.
---

1. Start your LIVE on TikTok.
2. Open Tokio, enter your `@username` on the **Home** page and connect.
3. Turn on the features you want for this stream: text-to-speech, music, alerts.

The **Home** page shows the live stats as viewers join, like and send gifts. Each feature can be turned on or off at any time during the session.

Tokio has to stay open while you stream so it keeps getting events and your overlays stay up to date.

## When something fails

When something goes wrong, such as a voice that can't be generated or a sound that's missing, Tokio shows a notification telling you what happened. The bell in the title bar keeps the session's history.
