Set everything up before you go live. Tokio can only connect once your LIVE has started.

<Steps>

1. **Install Tokio**

   See [Install](/docs/install/).

2. **Set it up**

   Paste your free [Euler Stream](https://www.eulerstream.com/register) API key in **Settings → TikTok LIVE**, once. Then set up your [voice](/docs/features/text-to-speech/), [music](/docs/features/music-requests/), [alerts](/docs/features/alerts/) and [overlays](/docs/features/overlays/).

   <Screenshot
     name="settings-tiktok-live"
     alt="The Euler Stream API key field in Settings → TikTok LIVE"
   />

3. **Prepare your stream**

   On **Home**, under **Active features**, turn on what you need; it stays as you leave it. [Add your overlays](/docs/guides/adding-an-overlay/) to your streaming app.

   <Screenshot
     name="home-features"
     alt="Active features on the Home page, with TTS and Alerts on and the overlay server running"
   />

   For music, click **Connect** on **Music** every time you start Tokio. It doesn't reconnect on its own, and **Home** shows "YouTube · not connected" until you do.

   For [games](/docs/features/games/), turn on the **Minecraft** switch on **Games** and paste its command in your world's chat.

4. **Go live and connect**

   Start your LIVE on TikTok. Then enter your `@username` on **Home** and click **Connect**.

   <Screenshot
     name="home-connect"
     alt="The connect box on Home, with @tokio.demo typed in and the Connect button"
   />

</Steps>

## While you're LIVE

**Home** shows how long you've been LIVE, **Viewers**, **Likes** and **Total views**, then **New followers**, **Shares**, **Gifts** and **Coins**. While you're connected, a rose line lights up at the top of the title bar.

<Screenshot
  name="dashboard"
  alt="The Home page during a LIVE: how long it's been on, the viewers, likes and views, the features that are on, and the activity feed"
/>

The **Activity** feed keeps the last 100 events. It stays after the LIVE ends, until the next one.

**Feed settings**, the gear next to **Activity**, picks which events the feed shows, and **Save** keeps your choice. Hidden events still count in the totals.

<Screenshot
  name="home-feed-settings"
  alt="The Activity feed window, with a switch for each kind of event"
/>

Viewers can also type [chat commands](/docs/features/chat-commands/), like `!play` to request a song.

Turning a feature off during the LIVE stops what it has queued. For music, that clears the requests, and the song playing keeps playing.

## When something fails

If Tokio can't connect, it tells you why. If the connection drops, it reconnects on its own, and tells you "Unable to reconnect to the LIVE. Connect again from Home." when it can't.

When the LIVE ends, Tokio disconnects with "The LIVE has ended." Any disconnect drops what TTS, alerts and music had queued, and the song playing keeps playing.

<details>
<summary>Why a connect fails</summary>

- The LIVE hasn't started yet.
- The username wasn't found on TikTok.
- Your Euler Stream key is missing, wasn't accepted or hit its rate limit.
- Your Euler Stream [plan](https://www.eulerstream.com/pricing) doesn't include the connection.
- Euler Stream couldn't sign it, or TikTok can't be reached.

</details>

Other problems show as notifications, kept under the bell in the title bar.
