Set everything up before you go live. Tokio can only connect to TikTok and YouTube once your LIVE has started.

<Steps>

1. **Install Tokio**

   See [Install](/docs/install/).

2. **Set it up**

   On **Home**, the **+** after the tabs adds a platform. For TikTok, paste your free [Euler Stream](https://www.eulerstream.com/register) API key in **Settings → TikTok LIVE**, once. For Twitch, [sign in](#sign-in-to-twitch) in **Settings → Twitch**. YouTube and Kick need neither. Then set up your [voice](/docs/features/text-to-speech/), [music](/docs/features/music-requests/), [alerts](/docs/features/alerts/) and [overlays](/docs/features/overlays/).

   <Screenshot
     name="settings-tiktok-live"
     alt="The Euler Stream API key field in Settings → TikTok LIVE"
   />

3. **Prepare your stream**

   On **Home**, under **Active features**, turn on what you need; they stay as you leave them. [Add your overlays](/docs/guides/adding-an-overlay/) to your streaming app.

   <Screenshot
     name="home-features"
     alt="Active features on the Home page, with TTS, Alerts and Overlays on"
   />

   For music, click **Connect** on **Music** each time you open Tokio. For [games](/docs/features/games/), turn on the **Minecraft** switch on **Games** and paste its command in your world's chat.

4. **Go live and connect**

   Start your LIVE. On **Home**, type your TikTok `@username`, your [YouTube](#connect-to-youtube) `@handle` or LIVE link, or your [Kick](#connect-to-kick) channel in its tab, then click **Connect**. Twitch uses the account you signed in with.

   <Screenshot
     name="home-connect"
     alt="Home before connecting: Offline, the TikTok tab with the + after it, @tokio.demo typed in and the Connect button"
   />

</Steps>

## Sign in to Twitch

Tokio connects to your own Twitch channel, so you sign in to it once.

<Steps>

1. In **Settings → Twitch** (there once Twitch has a tab on **Home**), click **Sign in with Twitch**. Tokio opens Twitch in your browser with a code already filled in.
2. Click **Activate** there to finish. Tokio then says **Signed in as @your-channel**.

</Steps>

Keep the code off your stream: anyone who types it signs Tokio in to their own account.

## Connect to YouTube

YouTube needs no key and no sign-in. In its tab, type your channel's `@handle` or paste a link to the LIVE, its chat or your channel. Tokio connects once the LIVE has started, with its chat turned on.

<details>
<summary>What YouTube sends, and its limits</summary>

Tokio takes the LIVE's watch, youtu.be, /live/, popout chat or YouTube Studio link, and your channel's. Before the LIVE starts, Tokio tells you the channel isn't LIVE. If the LIVE's chat is turned off, it asks you to turn it on in YouTube Studio.

Tokio reads the LIVE's chat from YouTube's public website, the way a browser does. It doesn't use an official API, so a change to YouTube's site can stop it from working until a Tokio update fixes it.

On YouTube, memberships count as subscriptions, gifted memberships as gifted subs, one event for the whole gift, and Gifts bought with Jewels as gifts. Super Chats and Super Stickers are their own event, **Super Chat**. YouTube doesn't send follows, since new channel subscribers don't show in its chat, nor likes, shares or total views.

Chat badges say who moderates and who is a member, with their months when the badge shows them.

</details>

## Connect to Kick

Kick needs no key and no sign-in either. Type your channel's name or paste its kick.com link. Tokio connects even before you go live, and disconnects when the stream ends.

<details>
<summary>What Kick sends, and its limits</summary>

Tokio also takes your channel's popout chat link. It disconnects at the stream's end only once it has seen the stream live.

Tokio reads Kick's website and chat feed the way kick.com's own page does, without an official API. A change on Kick's side can stop it from working until a Tokio update fixes it.

On Kick, Kicks are Kick's money, like Twitch's Bits: they come as their own event, **Kicks**, with how many the viewer sent and the message they pinned, if any. Kick sends no gifts. Subscriptions count as subscriptions, with their months, gifted subs as gifted subs, one event for the whole gift, hosts as raids, with the viewers they bring, and channel rewards as channel points. Kick's emotes count as chat emotes. Kick doesn't send follows, since its chat feed carries none, nor likes, shares or total views.

Chat badges say who moderates, who subscribes and who's a VIP, as on Twitch, with a subscriber's months when the badge shows them.

</details>

## Several platforms together

Each platform has its own tab on **Home**, and they all connect to the same LIVE. The **+** adds one, even while you're LIVE, and a tab's × removes it once that platform is disconnected. Drag a tab to change the order.

With two or more, **Connect all** connects every platform that's ready, and each tab's **Connect** works on its platform alone. If one fails or its LIVE ends, Tokio keeps going on the others. **Disconnect all** leaves them all.

| Events                        | Come from                        |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Chat                          | TikTok, Twitch, YouTube and Kick |
| Follows                       | TikTok and Twitch                |
| Gifts                         | TikTok and YouTube               |
| Subscriptions and gifted subs | Twitch, YouTube and Kick         |
| Raids and channel points      | Twitch and Kick                  |
| Likes, shares and Super Fans  | TikTok                           |
| Bits                          | Twitch                           |
| Super Chats                   | YouTube                          |
| Kicks                         | Kick                             |

Each platform's money counts on its own, never added to another's: see [LIVE history](/docs/features/coin-stats/).

## While you're LIVE

While you're connected, **Home** shows how long you've been LIVE and your **Viewers**, with a line of the LIVE so far and its **Peak**. Below them are the LIVE's numbers and who sent the most, for the platform whose tab is open.

<details>
<summary>What each tab shows</summary>

Each tab shows its platform's viewers right now next to its name. A platform you disconnect keeps its numbers in its tab until the LIVE ends.

| Tab     | Numbers                                                                         |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| TikTok  | **Likes**, **Total views**, **New followers**, **Shares**, **Gifts**, **Coins** |
| Twitch  | **New followers**, **Gifted subs**, **Bits**                                    |
| YouTube | **Gifts**, **Gifted subs**, **Super Chats**                                     |
| Kick    | **Gifted subs**, **Kicks**                                                      |

On two or more platforms, an **All** tab adds up the viewers, the counts and the gifted subs, with a tile for each platform's money.

Twitch's and YouTube's viewer counts update every 30 seconds, and Kick's every 60.

</details>

Viewers can also type [chat commands](/docs/features/chat-commands/), like `!play` to request a song.

Turning a feature off during the LIVE stops what it has queued. For music, that clears the requests, and the song playing keeps playing.

## When something fails

If Tokio can't connect, it tells you why. When the network was the problem, it first tries twice more, 3 seconds apart.

If the connection drops, Tokio reconnects on its own until it's back or you click **Disconnect**. If the LIVE ended meanwhile, **Home** says **Waiting for the LIVE…** and Tokio looks for it for 2 minutes.

Any disconnect drops what TTS, alerts and music had queued. The song playing keeps playing.

<details>
<summary>Why a connect fails</summary>

- The LIVE hasn't started yet.
- The username or channel wasn't found.
- The LIVE's chat is turned off on YouTube. Turn it on in YouTube Studio.
- Your Euler Stream key is missing or wasn't accepted, or you've connected too often for your plan.
- Your Euler Stream [plan](https://www.eulerstream.com/pricing) doesn't include the connection.
- Euler Stream couldn't sign it, or TikTok can't be reached.
- You aren't signed in to Twitch, or Twitch signed Tokio out. Sign in again in **Settings → Twitch**.
- Twitch, YouTube or Kick is limiting Tokio's requests: try again in a few minutes.
- The LIVE ended: Tokio says "The LIVE has ended."
- The LIVE didn't come back within those 2 minutes: Tokio says "Unable to reconnect to the LIVE. Connect again from Home."

</details>

Other problems show as notifications, under the bell in the title bar.
