Set everything up before you start your LIVE. Tokio can only connect once the LIVE has started.

<Steps>

1. **Install Tokio**

   Download it from the [download page](/download/). See [Install](/docs/install/) for each system.

2. **Set it up**

   Pick your [voices](/docs/features/text-to-speech/), connect your [music](/docs/features/music-requests/), choose your [alert sounds](/docs/features/alerts/) and [overlays](/docs/features/overlays/). Each [profile](/docs/features/profiles-and-settings/) keeps its own settings.

3. **Prepare your stream**

   On the **Home** page, turn on the features you want for this stream under **Active Features**: **TTS**, **Music** and **Alerts**. Then add your overlays to OBS or TikTok LIVE Studio; see [Adding an overlay](/docs/guides/adding-an-overlay/).

4. **Go live and connect**

   Start your LIVE on TikTok. Then enter your `@username` on the **Home** page and click **Connect**.

</Steps>

While you're LIVE, the **Home** page shows how long you've been on, the viewers, likes and views, and the new followers, shares, gifts and coins since you connected. Its **Activity** feed lists chats, gifts, follows, shares and viewers joining as they come in. The gear next to **Activity** picks which of them it shows. Each feature can be turned on or off at any time during the session.

Tokio has to stay open while you stream so it keeps getting events and your overlays stay up to date.

## When something fails

If Tokio can't connect, check the username and that your LIVE has started.

When something else goes wrong, such as a voice that can't be generated or a sound that's missing, Tokio shows a notification telling you what happened. The bell in the title bar keeps the session's history.
